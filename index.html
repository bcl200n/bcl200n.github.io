About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Architecture at Tongji University with more than 10 years of higher-education teaching and research experience. My research focuses on multi-agent reinforcement learning, seismic resilience assessment, urban renewal, and AI4Science. I am currently exploring open-source AI, world models, and their applications in urban safety, resilience planning, and disaster emergency response.

I formerly served as a Senior Lecturer at the School of Tourism and Urban-Rural Planning, Xichang University, and founded Yiren Architecture and Landscape Design Co., Ltd. My work connects higher education, ecological planning and design, digital spatial technologies, and applied AI systems.
