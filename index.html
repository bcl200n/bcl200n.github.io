城市雷达与数据分析系统City Radar and Data Analysis System

面向城市空间大数据、人口分布可视分析和 GIS 报告生成的科研转化软件。软件“城市雷达与数据技术平台V1.0”已于2026年9月30日登记（2026SR0999998），本人为共同著作权人。

Research translation software for urban spatial big data, population visualization, and GIS reporting. City Radar and Data Technology Platform V1.0 was registered on 30 September 2026 (2026SR0999998); I am a joint copyright holder.

面向城市空间大数据推演与人口分布可视分析的企业级平台

近期开发了“城市雷达与数据分析系统”，以凉山彝族自治州为核心研究区域，融合多源时空数据与高性能 WebGL 渲染引擎，实现人口热力点阵的实时 3D 推演、人口多维画像统计、2D/3D 空间分析和标准化 GIS 报告自动生成。

该系统采用 B/S 前后端分离架构，后端使用 Python + FastAPI，前端结合 MapLibre GL JS、Deck.gl、Chart.js、jsPDF 和 html2canvas，支持 Windows 10/11 环境部署，并可适配政务内网的离线运行场景。当前软件著作权正在申请中。

An enterprise platform for urban spatial big-data simulation and population distribution analysis

I recently developed the City Radar and Data Analysis System, using Liangshan Yi Autonomous Prefecture as the core study area. The system integrates multi-source spatiotemporal data with high-performance WebGL rendering to support real-time 3D population heatmap simulation, multidimensional demographic profiling, 2D/3D spatial analysis, and automated GIS report generation.

The system uses a B/S architecture with Python + FastAPI on the backend and MapLibre GL JS, Deck.gl, Chart.js, jsPDF, and html2canvas on the frontend. It supports Windows 10/11 deployment and can be adapted to offline government intranet environments.

股伴AI · 应用型 AI 原型StockMate AI · Applied AI Prototype

将多智能体思路延伸到 AI + 金融产品探索的个人项目。

A side project extending multi-agent thinking into AI + finance product exploration.

多智能体投资合议庭：帮助普通投资者整理信息，而不是替代决策

股伴AI 是一个 AI 辅助分析原型。它不直接给出“买还是不买”的结论，而是让宏观、行业、财报、技术、舆情、风控和策略等多个智能体分别提出观点、交叉质询，最后形成一份综合分析报告。

这个项目延续了我对多智能体系统、信息组织和决策支持的兴趣，目标是降低普通投资者面对公告、新闻、政策、技术指标、财报和社交情绪时的信息处理成本。

A multi-agent investment council for organizing information, not replacing decisions

StockMate AI is an AI-assisted analysis prototype. Instead of directly saying whether to buy or not, it lets multiple agents from macro, industry, financial-report, technical, sentiment, risk-control, and strategy perspectives present views, question one another, and produce a synthesized analysis report.

The project extends my interest in multi-agent systems, information organization, and decision support, reducing the information-processing cost faced by ordinary investors.
