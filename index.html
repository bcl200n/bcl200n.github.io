About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Architecture at Tongji University. My research focuses on multi-agent reinforcement learning, seismic resilience assessment, urban renewal, and AI4Science.

I formerly served as a Senior Lecturer at the School of Tourism and Urban-Rural Planning, Xichang University, and founded Yiren Architecture and Landscape Design Co., Ltd. My work connects higher education, ecological planning and design, digital spatial technologies, and applied AI systems.

中文简介：同济大学建筑学博士研究生，研究方向聚焦多智能体强化学习、抗震韧性评价与城市更新、AI4Science方向。拥有10年以上高校教学、生态规划设计与数字技术应用研究经验。
